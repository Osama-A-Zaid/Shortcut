# Shortcut
### Business Onboarding, Simplified.

**Shortcut is a B2B SaaS platform designed to simplify how businesses onboard, manage, and collaborate with external partners.**

We help organizations reduce repetitive paperwork, centralize business information, and streamline partner onboarding through reusable digital business identities and structured workflows.

---

## 1. The Problem

Businesses repeatedly exchange the same documents and information when onboarding external partners.

For example:
- Real estate developers onboarding brokers and brokerage companies.
- Businesses onboarding sellers, distributors, and external representatives.
- Partners submitting similar documents to multiple organizations.
- Teams managing onboarding through emails, WhatsApp, and spreadsheets.

This creates duplicated work, fragmented communication, and unnecessary delays.

**The same business information is repeatedly requested, submitted, and reviewed.**

## 2. Our Solution

Shortcut creates a centralized environment for business identity, document sharing, and onboarding coordination.

Our platform aims to enable businesses to:

- Create reusable digital business profiles.
- Organize and maintain company documents.
- Share information with external organizations.
- Complete structured onboarding workflows.
- Track application status and outstanding requirements.
- Reuse relevant information across business relationships.

## 3. Target Users

**External Partners**
- Real estate brokers.

- Sellers and distributors.
- Independent business representatives.

**Initial Market:** UAE real estate, focusing on developer–broker onboarding.

**Long-term Vision:** Expand into other industries with recurring business onboarding requirements.

## 4. How Shortcut Works

1. **Create Identity:** A business creates its digital profile and organizes its documents.
2. **Connect:** The business receives an onboarding request from another organization.
3. **Share:** Relevant information and documents are submitted securely.
4. **Review:** The receiving organization reviews the submission and decides whether to approve, reject, or request additional information.
5. **Track:** Both parties can follow the onboarding progress.
6. **Reuse:** The business can reuse its profile and relevant documents for future onboarding, with appropriate permission.

## 5. Core Product Modules

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Business Identity | Reusable digital business profiles |
| Document Management | Organizing and sharing business documents |
| Onboarding Workflows | Managing structured onboarding processes |
| Partner Collaboration | Connecting businesses and external partners |
| Status Tracking | Monitoring onboarding progress |
| AI Document Intelligence | Assisting with document identification and data extraction |

*AI capabilities and other advanced modules are planned features, not necessarily available in the current prototype.*

## 6. Product Principles

- **Simplicity:** Make complex onboarding easier.
- **Reusability:** Reduce repetitive information collection.
- **Security:** Give businesses control over information sharing.
- **Transparency:** Make requirements and progress visible.
- **Scalability:** Support different industries and workflows.

Shortcut facilitates onboarding coordination and document exchange. The receiving organization remains responsible for document verification, compliance reviews, and final approval decisions.
